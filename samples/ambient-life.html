Ambient life

Ideas for the small life between the events: ships, caravans and fishing boats, on the same pieces of map as the seasons. They move on the wall clock, so they read the same at every speed, but come and go on the calendar: fewer ships where the sea freezes, no boats on frozen water, caravans waiting out deep snow. Each panel has a date, a speed and held (the year-round look when the years fly by). ?only=3 shows one panel large.
