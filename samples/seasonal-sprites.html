Seasonal sprites

The seasons on the sprites as well as the land. The land is drawn as in the seasons (3 screen pixels a map pixel), and over it the app's own sprites at the settlements' scale (2 screen pixels a sprite pixel, as spriteUnit gives a little zoomed in): peaks, trees, pines and palms one per map cell where the app puts them, the village and the ports. The trees turn and go bare, the snow lies on the peaks, the pines, the bare branches and the roofs, the orchard is a sprite in rows, the fields get sheaves after the harvest, and the ships, boats and caravans are sprites that go home before the ice or snow. Each panel has a date, a speed and held (the look when the years go faster than one a second). ?only=4 shows one panel large.
