Seasons

Ideas for the land and sea through the year, on small pieces of map in the game's own palette. Every pixel carries what a map cell knows (coldest and warmest month, rain, summer rain, biome, height), and each effect is decided from that alone. Every panel keeps what the map has now: the sea's foam, the river current, and the lake and river ice of pixelmap.js, the reference for the calendar. Each panel runs on its own calendar: drag the date, pick a speed (the app's speeds), or tick held for the look the map keeps when the years go by faster than one a second. The bar below sets all panels at once. ?only=4 shows one panel large; ?day=300&speed=0 starts every panel on a date and speed.
