Settlement sprites, round 2

A new walled city, and settlements by water: a harbour on the coast, a bridge or mill on a river, a jetty or stilts on a lake. Every panel is drawn on the map's own ground at the map's scale (one map pixel is 3 screen pixels, one sprite pixel 2, as at the usual zoom), shown twice as large above and at true size below. Water pieces sit on the side the water is; on the map they flip when the water is on the left. ?only=N shows panel N large.
