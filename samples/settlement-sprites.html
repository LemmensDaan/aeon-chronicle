Settlement sprites, round 3

Every roof is now in its realm's colour, as the capital's was, shaded on its right-hand slope. The settlement panels show three bands: a blue realm on plain ground (the biome map), the same realm under its political wash, and a red realm under its wash. The keep and bailey is the capital. Water panels use the town and city now on the map, to be swapped for the picks. One map pixel is 3 screen pixels and one sprite pixel 2, as at the usual zoom; each panel is shown twice as large, then a close-up, then true size. ?only=N shows panel N large.
