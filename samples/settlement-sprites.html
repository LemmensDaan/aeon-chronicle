Settlement sprites: the chosen set

Daan's picks after round 5: the wide keep with its banner is the capital (now centred on its wall and gate), the belfry over a low wall is the city, the stockade is the town, and every roof is in its realm's colour. By the water: the quay with a light at its tip and the realm's own ship bobbing beside it, a watermill or a bridge on a river depending on whether a road crosses, stilt houses and boats on a lake. The other keep, the hill city, the stockade with a lookout, the cathedral, the gabled row and the windmill are kept at the end as extra designs. The settlement panels show three bands: a blue realm on plain ground, the same realm under its political wash, a red realm under its wash.
